# Chilean Inflation Monitor
**Last updated:** 10-26-2026

This project analyzes inflation dynamics in Chile using disaggregated consumer price data. It combines an assessment of recent price developments with estimates of persistent inflation and forecasts across multiple horizons.

The analysis is organized around three components:

- **Inflation developments.** Analysis of headline and sectoral inflation, including the contributions of individual categories to aggregate price changes.
- **Inflation persistence.** Estimation of underlying inflation using a multivariate model that distinguishes common and sector-specific persistent components.
- **Inflation forecasting.** Forecasts informed by disaggregated inflation and additional economic indicators, supported by benchmark comparisons and an assessment of forecast uncertainty.

## Methodological notes

Each report identifies its information cutoff and the data available at the time of preparation. Estimates of persistent inflation and forecasts of future CPI describe different quantities; their interpretation and uncertainty are documented separately.

Historical forecast evaluations use information truncated at each forecast origin. Where complete historical data vintages are unavailable, the evaluation is described as pseudo-real-time. Model choices, assumptions, diagnostics, and limitations are documented alongside the results.
